Terms of Service

1. The service

The Partner Portal is a backoffice for a single tenant. It lets authorised operators invite end users, review cardholder applications, issue and freeze cards, monitor card and crypto transactions, and manage float, custody balances, webhooks and API keys.

2. Your account

Accounts are issued to named individuals. You are responsible for everything done under your credentials, and for keeping them secret. Tell us immediately if you believe an account or API key has been exposed — keys can be rolled from the API keys screen at any time.

3. Acceptable use

  • Act only within the authority your organisation has granted you.
  • Do not attempt to reach records belonging to another tenant.
  • Do not probe, load-test or reverse engineer the service without written permission.
  • Respect the published API rate limit; sustained excess may be throttled.

4. Live and sandbox

Sandbox is isolated from production: balances, cards and transactions there carry no monetary value. Actions taken while the environment switcher reads Live affect real cardholders and real money. Check the switcher before you act.

5. Availability and liability

We aim for continuous availability but do not guarantee it. The service is provided as-is; nothing here creates liability for indirect or consequential loss beyond what your commercial agreement states.

Privacy Policy

What we process

  • Operator data — your name, work email, role, and a log of the actions you take.
  • End user and cardholder data — reference identifiers, KYC decision outcomes and rejection reasons supplied by the issuer.
  • Transaction data — card authorisations, crypto deposits, withdrawals, settlements and float movements.
  • Technical data — IP address, API request paths, response codes and latency.

Why

To operate the card programme, meet anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer obligations, investigate disputes, and keep the service secure. The audit log exists so privileged actions are attributable.

Retention

Financial and KYC records are retained for the period financial-services regulation requires. Operator session data is kept for 30 days when “Remember me” is used and 12 hours otherwise.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your operator data. Erasure may be refused where a record must be retained for regulatory reasons. Write to service@travls.io.

Cookies and storage

No advertising or analytics cookies are set. The panel keeps two items in browser storage: your session token and your light/dark theme choice. Clearing site data signs you out.
